# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and provide meaningful insights into workforce demographics, attrition patterns, job satisfaction, and departmental performance.

The dashboard helps HR teams and management understand employee trends and make data driven decisions related to workforce planning, retention strategies, and organizational performance.

## Key Metrics

The dashboard provides high level KPIs for quick HR insights:

* Total Employees: 398
* Active Employees: 340
* Employee Attrition: 58
* Attrition Rate: 14.57 percent
* Average Employee Age: 39 years

These indicators allow HR managers to quickly evaluate workforce health and attrition trends.

---

## Key Insights Visualized

### Department wise Attrition

A pie chart visualizing employee attrition across departments:

* Research and Development
* Sales
* Human Resources

This helps identify which department contributes most to employee turnover.

### Employee Distribution by Age Group

A bar chart showing workforce distribution across age groups:

* Under 25
* 25 to 34
* 35 to 44
* 45 to 54
* Over 55

The visualization also compares male and female employee counts.

### Job Satisfaction Analysis

A detailed matrix showing job satisfaction ratings across different job roles such as:

* Sales Executive
* Research Scientist
* Laboratory Technician
* Healthcare Representative
* Manager
* Human Resources

This allows HR teams to understand satisfaction trends by role.

### Attrition by Education Field

A horizontal bar chart highlighting attrition based on educational background such as:

* Life Sciences
* Medical
* Marketing
* Technical Degree
* Human Resources

This provides insights into which education backgrounds experience higher attrition.

### Attrition Rate by Age and Gender

Donut charts analyze attrition across different age groups and genders, helping identify demographic segments with higher employee turnover.
